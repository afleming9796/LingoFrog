Anthropic API key

Scribe writes drafts with Claude using your own Anthropic API key (create one at console.anthropic.com). The key is stored only on this device. When you click Write, your context, general style and chosen style, saved links, and top phrases are sent to Anthropic's API. Asking for a suggested rule also sends the original and edited draft. Nothing is sent before you click.
